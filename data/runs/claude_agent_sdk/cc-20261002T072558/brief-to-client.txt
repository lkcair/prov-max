Lisbon currently enjoys a pleasant 21 °C with clear skies, perched on the Tagus estuary where the river meets the Atlantic. Its historic Alfama district, a UNESCO World Heritage site, winds up the hillside above the river, featuring Fado houses, São Jorge Castle, and the iconic Belém Tower, reflecting the city’s origins as a 2nd‑century BC Phoenician trading post and its golden age during the Age of Discovery.
